1. Introduction

picex uses cookies and similar technologies to deliver a secure, reliable and personalized trading experience. These technologies help us keep users logged in, protect accounts, remember preferences, improve performance, monitor errors and support platform functionality.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They can be used to maintain sessions, store preferences, improve security, analyze usage and enhance the user experience.

picex may also use similar technologies, including:

  • localStorage for interface settings and user preferences.
  • sessionStorage for temporary browser session data.
  • Device or browser identifiers for risk detection.
  • Authentication tokens for login and API requests.
  • WebSocket session identifiers for real-time market data.

3. Types of Cookies and Similar Technologies

Type Purpose Required?
Essential Cookies Login, session management, security and core website functionality. Yes
Security Cookies Suspicious login detection, fraud prevention, device and IP checks. Yes
Preference Cookies Language, theme, chart settings, simple/pro mode and interface preferences. No, but they improve user experience.
Analytics Cookies Understanding platform usage, identifying errors and improving product design. Depending on user consent and applicable law.
Support Cookies Maintaining chat state, ticket history and support continuity. Depending on support usage.
Third-Party Cookies Infrastructure, security, KYC, analytics, messaging, AI or support tools. Depends on the service.

4. Login, Security and Pi Integration

picex may use cookies, tokens or browser storage to manage user login, protect accounts and support integrations with Pi-related authentication or payment flows where applicable.

  • Maintaining user login status.
  • Authenticating API requests.
  • Preventing unauthorized access.
  • Detecting suspicious devices, sessions or locations.
  • Supporting secure interaction with Pi SDK or other supported authentication services.
If you disable essential cookies or storage, you may be unable to log in, place orders, withdraw assets or use core picex services.

5. Analytics, Performance and Product Improvement

picex may use analytics data to improve speed, reliability, user flows, design, error handling and service quality.

This may include:

  • Visited pages and session duration.
  • Browser type, operating system and screen size.
  • JavaScript errors, API errors or WebSocket disruptions.
  • General usage paths for Deposit, Withdraw, Trade and Support features.
  • Aggregated product metrics for user experience improvement.

Where required by law, picex may request your consent before using non-essential analytics cookies.

6. Cookies and Internal Market Data Experience

Since picex intends to generate price charts and market data from its own internal trading activity, browser storage may be used to improve the trading interface.

  • Remembering the last selected market, such as PI-USDT.
  • Remembering chart intervals, such as 1m, 5m or 1h.
  • Saving order book and chart display preferences.
  • Improving real-time WebSocket market data experience.

These values are generally used for convenience and should never include private keys, seed phrases or highly sensitive information.

7. Online Support and AI

picex may use session identifiers, chat state, ticket history and support-related storage to provide online support and AI-assisted responses.

  • Maintaining support chat continuity.
  • Connecting a support conversation to a logged-in account.
  • Helping AI respond based on picex documentation, policies and FAQs.
  • Improving support quality and identifying cases that require human review.
Never enter private keys, seed phrases, passwords, 2FA codes or highly sensitive credentials into support chat or AI support.

8. Third-Party Services

picex may use third-party services for hosting, security, analytics, KYC, messaging, support, AI, error monitoring or blockchain infrastructure. These services may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Categories of third-party providers may include:

  • Infrastructure and CDN providers.
  • Security and anti-fraud providers.
  • KYC/AML service providers.
  • Error monitoring and performance analytics tools.
  • Support and AI service providers.

Use of these providers should be consistent with the picex Privacy Policy and applicable legal requirements.

9. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. If picex provides a cookie consent or preference panel, you may also manage non-essential cookies there.

  • In Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies.
  • In Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • In Safari: Preferences → Privacy.
  • On mobile browsers, cookie settings are usually located under Privacy settings.
Disabling essential cookies may break login, security, trading, withdrawals, charts or support features.

10. Retention

Cookie retention depends on the type of cookie or storage item.

  • Session cookies may be deleted when the browser is closed or when you log out.
  • Login cookies may remain for a limited period to keep you signed in.
  • Security and anti-fraud cookies may be retained as required for account protection and legal obligations.
  • Analytics data may be retained in aggregated or pseudonymized form for product improvement.
