Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PiDao respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PiDao, we may collect basic information provided through Pi Network authentication, such as your Pi user ID and username.

We may also collect interaction data related to voting, tasks, payments, and usage of the application.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide authentication, prevent duplicate voting, process Pi-related interactions, display user participation, and improve the platform.

3. Pi Network Authentication

PiDao uses Pi Network tools such as Pi SDK for user authentication and participation. We do not collect or store your Pi wallet private keys.

4. Payments

If Pi payments are used, payment-related information such as payment ID, transaction status, and transaction identifiers may be processed to complete and verify transactions.

5. Data Storage

Some application data may be stored in our backend systems or database, including voting records and user participation data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data only when required to operate the application, comply with legal obligations, or interact with Pi Network services.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect user data. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. DIG Vision

PiDao is an early infrastructure related to the DIG vision. At this stage, DIG is a concept and development path for transparent and people-powered participation.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the PiDao project team through the official project communication channels.
