Important warning: You may lose part or all of your funds. picex does not guarantee profit, price stability, liquidity, uninterrupted access, successful withdrawals in all circumstances, or the future value of any digital asset.

This document is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. You should consult an independent professional advisor if you are unsure whether digital asset trading is suitable for you.

2. General Digital Asset Risk

  • Digital assets can be highly volatile and may lose value rapidly.
  • Transactions on blockchain networks are usually irreversible.
  • A digital asset may become illiquid, unsupported, restricted or legally unavailable.
  • Incorrect transfers may result in permanent loss of assets.
  • Past market performance does not indicate future results.

3. Market, Price and Liquidity Risk

Prices displayed on picex may be based on the platform’s own order book, trades and liquidity. They may differ from prices on external exchanges or data providers.

  • Low liquidity may cause high slippage.
  • Market orders may execute at prices worse than expected.
  • Limit orders are not guaranteed to execute fully or at all.
  • Extreme volatility may cause delayed execution, wider spreads or temporary market restrictions.
  • picex does not guarantee that a buyer or seller will always be available.

4. Spot Trading Risk

Spot trading involves buying and selling digital assets directly. Even without leverage, spot trading can result in significant loss.

  • The asset price may decrease after purchase.
  • You may be unable to sell quickly at your desired price.
  • Fees and slippage may reduce your final return.
  • Internal market conditions on picex may differ from external exchanges.

5. Futures, Perpetual and Leverage Risk

If picex enables futures, perpetual contracts or leveraged products in the future, those products will involve substantially higher risk than spot trading.

Leveraged trading can lead to rapid liquidation and the loss of your entire margin or collateral.
  • Leverage magnifies both profits and losses.
  • Small adverse price movements may trigger liquidation.
  • Funding rates, liquidation fees, index price differences and market disruptions may affect your result.
  • picex may restrict, adjust, settle or close positions in emergency conditions.
  • Derivatives may only be available to eligible, KYC-verified users in permitted jurisdictions.

7. Deposit and Withdrawal Risk

7.1 Deposits

  • Deposits are credited only after detection, validation and the required confirmation policy.
  • Before final crediting, deposits may appear as Pending.
  • Sending assets to the wrong address, wrong network, wrong memo/tag or unsupported asset may be unrecoverable.
  • picex may delay or block deposit crediting for suspicious or non-compliant transactions.

7.2 Withdrawals

  • You are responsible for the correctness of the destination address, network, memo/tag and amount.
  • Once broadcast to the network, withdrawals may be irreversible.
  • Withdrawals may be delayed due to security checks, KYC/AML review, hot wallet liquidity, network congestion or manual review.
  • Large withdrawals, new destinations or unusual behavior may require additional approval.

8. Custody, Hot Wallet and Cold Wallet Risk

User balances on picex may be recorded in an internal ledger. picex may use hot wallets for operational withdrawals and cold wallets for reserve storage.

  • All digital asset custody systems involve operational and security risk.
  • Hot wallets are more exposed than cold wallets because they support online operations.
  • Cold wallet transfers may delay large withdrawals because they require stricter controls.
  • In security incidents, picex may temporarily pause withdrawals or restrict wallet operations.
