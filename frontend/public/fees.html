1. Overview

picex uses a maker/taker fee model. This model is designed to reward liquidity providers and keep trading costs transparent.

  • Maker: A user who adds liquidity by placing an order that rests on the order book.
  • Taker: A user who removes liquidity by executing against an existing order.
picex aims to offer low and competitive fees. However, any statement about “lowest fees” should not be interpreted as a permanent guarantee against all exchanges or market conditions.

2. User Levels

picex uses a simple two-level user model for access, risk controls and fee policy.

User Level Description Benefits / Restrictions
Unverified A user without completed or verifiable KYC status. Limited access, lower withdrawal limits and higher fees than verified users.
KYC-Verified A user whose identity or KYC status has been verified. Lower fees, higher limits and broader access to platform features.

7. Network Fees and Variable Costs

Network fees are determined by the relevant blockchain or settlement infrastructure. They may change due to congestion, transaction type, asset, chain conditions or provider policy.

picex may dynamically adjust withdrawal fees to avoid operational loss or failed transactions. The final fee must be shown before submission.

8. Market Maker Rebates and Incentives

picex may offer reduced maker fees or rebates to approved market makers in order to improve order book depth and reduce user slippage.

  • Rebates are available only to approved and eligible accounts.
  • Market makers may be required to maintain volume, spread and uptime commitments.
  • picex may change, suspend or terminate market maker programs.
  • Manipulative activity such as spoofing, layering or wash trading may result in disqualification, fee reversal, suspension or account closure.

9. API, Market Data and Premium Services

picex may charge separate fees for premium APIs, institutional access, advanced reporting, professional market data or enterprise services.

  • Public APIs may be free but subject to rate limits.
  • Professional or institutional APIs may require subscription or approval.
  • Internal market data generated by picex may be subject to data usage rules.

10. Fee Changes

picex may update fees at any time due to market conditions, network costs, legal requirements, risk controls or product changes.

Material changes may be communicated through the website, app, official announcements or fee page. Continued use of picex means you accept the updated fees.
