Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how picex collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Pi Network focused payment, wallet, governance, market data, AI support, and future exchange-related services.

Last updated: January 2026

picex is under active development. Some features described in this policy may be planned, experimental, or not yet available in production. This policy will be updated as the platform evolves.

1. Introduction

picex is a Pi-first hybrid exchange and payment experience designed for the Pi Network ecosystem. Our services may include Pi login, Pi payment flows, wallet access, governance polls, native market data, AI support, and future trading-related features.

By using picex, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the platform.

2. Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use picex and which features are available at the time.

Category Examples Purpose
Pi account information Pi user ID, Pi username, Pi authentication response, Pi access token if provided by Pi SDK To authenticate users, create sessions, and enable Pi-based access.
Payment information Payment ID, transaction ID/TXID, amount, order ID, payment status, network type, timestamps To approve, complete, record, and verify Pi payment flows.
Wallet-related data Pending balance records, deposit/withdrawal requests, internal ledger entries, wallet operation status To support wallet, deposit, withdrawal, and reconciliation features when available.

Support and AI interaction data Questions, support messages, issue descriptions, AI assistant prompts, support metadata To provide user support, answer questions, improve documentation, and resolve issues.
Future trading data Orders, trades, balances, market interactions, chart data, order book activity To operate trading, market data, and exchange-related features if and when they become available.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users through Pi Network login flows.
  • To approve, complete, verify, and record Pi payments.
  • To provide wallet access and future deposit/withdrawal functionality.
  • To operate governance polls and prevent duplicate voting.
  • To provide customer support and AI-assisted guidance.
  • To maintain platform security and detect abuse, fraud, or suspicious activity.
  • To debug errors, monitor availability, and improve performance.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, security, or platform requirements.
  • To develop future market, trading, charting, and exchange infrastructure.

4. Pi Network and Third-Party Services

picex uses or may use the official Pi Network SDK and Pi Network Developer Platform to provide Pi login and payment functionality. When you authenticate or create a payment through Pi, certain information may be processed by Pi Network according to its own policies and developer platform rules.

picex may also use infrastructure providers, database providers, hosting services, analytics tools, security tools, or support services to operate and improve the platform. These providers may process limited information only as necessary to deliver their services.

5. Authentication Tokens and Local Storage

picex may store authentication tokens, user profile data, and language preferences in your browser using local storage or similar browser storage technologies. This helps keep you logged in and remembers your selected language.

You can clear local storage through your browser settings. Clearing local storage may log you out or reset your preferences.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

picex may use cookies or similar technologies for session management, security, analytics, performance monitoring, and user experience improvements. If analytics or tracking tools are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only in the following cases:

  • With service providers that help operate hosting, storage, security, payments, support, or infrastructure.
  • With Pi Network services when required for Pi login or Pi payment functionality.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of picex, users, or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of platform assets.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls, secure API design, backend validation, token-based authentication, and controlled database access.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including security, payment verification, audit, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and platform operation.

Payment records, governance votes, wallet records, and security logs may be retained for longer periods where needed for auditability, fraud prevention, or compliance.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to:

  • Access the information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data where applicable.

Some information may need to be retained for security, payment verification, legal, or operational reasons.

11. AI Support and User Messages

picex may provide AI-assisted support features. If you submit questions, issue descriptions, or support messages, we may process that content to generate responses, improve documentation, troubleshoot issues, and escalate sensitive cases to human support.

Do not submit passwords, private keys, seed phrases, wallet secrets, or sensitive personal documents through AI support unless explicitly requested through a secure official process.

12. Wallet, Trading, and Compliance Considerations

picex may introduce wallet, deposit, withdrawal, market data, spot trading, futures-ready, or other exchange-related functionality over time. Such features may require additional verification, compliance checks, risk controls, or user limits.

If KYC, compliance screening, fraud prevention, or regulatory checks become required, additional data may be collected and this policy may be updated.

13. Children’s Privacy

picex is not intended for children or users who are not permitted to use Pi Network, payment, wallet, or exchange-related services under applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. International Use

picex may be accessed from different countries. Your information may be processed in jurisdictions where our infrastructure, service providers, or databases operate. By using picex, you understand that information may be processed outside your country of residence.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as picex evolves, new features are added, or legal and platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
